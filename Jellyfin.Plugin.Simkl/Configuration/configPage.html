Scrobbling options

Marking played or unplayed in Jellyfin changes the Simkl history too. Playback is scrobbled either way.
Needs a Simkl Pro or VIP plan. Upgrade at simkl.com/vip.
Movies and episodes with a shorter runtime are never scrobbled. 5 by default.
  • Play, pause and stop are reported to Simkl as they happen and show on the profile as watching now; who can see that is set under Watching now privacy on Simkl.
  • An item is marked as watched when playback stops after 80%; stopping earlier saves the position on Simkl, where the Playback Progress Manager lists it.
  • With manual marks on, marking a movie, an episode, a season or a whole series as played in Jellyfin marks it as watched on Simkl too, and unmarking a single movie or episode also removes it from the Simkl history. Unmarking a whole season or series does not touch the Simkl history, by design, so a bulk change in Jellyfin cannot wipe out a watch history.
  • With rewatches on, finishing something that is already in the Simkl history is logged as a rewatch; this needs a Simkl Pro or VIP account and is ignored otherwise.

Import history

Marks movies and episodes in the Jellyfin library as played when they are watched on Simkl, with the date they were watched. Nothing is sent from Jellyfin to Simkl.
Jellyfin asks Simkl whether this user watched anything new and marks only those items. The very first import goes through the whole history.

Start over

Goes through the whole Simkl history again, for example when the import missed something or the user now has a different Simkl account. Nothing is unmarked: movies and episodes Jellyfin already has as played only get their watch date refreshed from Simkl.
